# Errata 2 — evidence brief of 8 September 2026

*The Too Hard Basket · Australian Public Interest Alliance · issued 14 September 2026*
*Supersedes the errata sheet of 13 September, which this one includes.*

**Please do not quote any recommendation-level figure from the brief.** Every one
of them has moved. The closure figures — 624 documents, 260 form letters, 2,266
template sentences, the 886 floor, 13 August 2024 — are unchanged and still
stand, as do both registers, the compliance record and the children-overboard
timeline.

## What happened

The index was missing 337 recommendations across 241 of the 624 response
documents, 218 of them the document's own Recommendation 1.

The cause was a rule written to keep cross-references out. When a response says
"implementing recommendation 7 from the Tax dispute inquiry report", that
mention of a number is not the heading of a recommendation being quoted, and the
index tested for the difference by asking whether the character before the label
ended a sentence. Once a PDF's line breaks are stripped out, a section heading
ends in a lower-case letter too. So

> …addresses the recommendations contained in the Report and in the additional
> comments. **Committee's Recommendations Recommendation 1** The committee
> recommends that the minimum consultation period…

read as the middle of a sentence, and the first recommendation of the document
was dropped without trace.

It was found on 14 September by hand, while checking a sample of the index
against the source documents. The reader asked how he could be sure the words
shown under one recommendation were not the answer to a different one — and the
document he picked to make the point, the response on the Privacy and Other
Legislation Amendment Bill 2024, turned out to be missing its Recommendation 1.

Every recovered row went through the same verification as every other row in the
index: found word for word in the document it names, under its own number. The
verifier kept 5,463 of 5,464 rows.

**The correction does not flatter the site.** Coverage moves from 29.4 per cent
to 29.6 — what was missing was spread across answered and unanswered alike. The
figure that changes most is the size of the index, not the share of it the
government answered.

Full account: toohardbasket.org.au/corrections/ (14 September 2026).

## The corrected figures

The two paragraphs to replace, with the new numbers in place:

> Read recommendation by recommendation, the responses state a position on
> **1,111** of the **3,749** recommendations they cover: **30 per cent**.

> **3,749** recommendations across **462** responses, each matched to the
> government's words about it. **1,111** of the **3,749**, **30 per cent**,
> received a stated position. Of those, **553** were accepted outright, **467**
> in part or in principle, and **91** were not accepted. The other **2,638** were
> noted without a position (**608**), closed with the form letter (**1,574**), or
> passed over by a response that addressed the report as a whole and never
> reached the recommendations (**456**). Of the **462** responses, **256** took a
> position on nothing the committee asked; **61** took one on everything.

Coverage by year:

| Year | Recommendations | Stated position | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 93 | 79 | 85 per cent |
| 2023 | 366 | 273 | 75 per cent |
| 2024 | 1,888 | 195 | 10 per cent |
| 2025 | 372 | 155 | 42 per cent |
| 2026 (to 3 September) | 1,030 | 409 | 40 per cent |

Note 2022 falls from 88 to 85 per cent and 2026 rises from 38 to 40. The shape
of the series is unchanged: 2024 is still the form-letter year at 10 per cent.

## Reproduce this

    git clone https://github.com/toohardbasket-lab/toohardbasket
    python scraper/check_brief.py 2026-09-08

The brief's text and a list of its claims are in `docs/briefs/`. The command
prints the table above from the current dataset. It is the command that produced
it.

A figure moving because the register moved is not a correction. This one is a
correction: the site was wrong, and it is listed, dated and explained at
toohardbasket.org.au/corrections/.
