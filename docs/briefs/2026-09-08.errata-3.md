# Errata 3 — evidence brief of 8 September 2026

*The Too Hard Basket · Australian Public Interest Alliance · issued 15 September 2026*
*Supersedes the errata sheets of 13 and 14 September. Use this one only.*

**Please do not quote any recommendation-level figure from the brief.** Every one
of them has moved, and so have the register and compliance counts. The closure
figures — 260 form-letter closures, 2,266 template sentences, 102 closing a
report in one line, the 886 floor across 81 closures, 39 responses on 13 August
2024 and 392 sentences, 205/4/51 by year, 59 and 201 on the bills point, 45
being considered, 5.1 per cent and 416 days — are unchanged and still stand, as
does the whole children-overboard timeline including 8,561 days.

Two different things moved the numbers, and they are not the same kind of thing.

## 1. A correction: 337 recommendations were missing

The index was missing 337 recommendations across 241 of the response documents,
218 of them the document's own Recommendation 1.

The cause was a rule written to keep cross-references out. When a response says
"implementing recommendation 7 from the Tax dispute inquiry report", that
mention of a number is not the heading of a recommendation being quoted, and the
index tested for the difference by asking whether the character before the label
ended a sentence. Once a PDF's line breaks are stripped out, a section heading
ends in a lower-case letter too. So

> …addresses the recommendations contained in the Report and in the additional
> comments. **Committee's Recommendations Recommendation 1** The committee
> recommends that the minimum consultation period…

read as the middle of a sentence, and the first recommendation of the document
was dropped without trace.

It was found on 14 September by hand, while checking a sample of the index
against the source documents. The reader asked how he could be sure the words
shown under one recommendation were not the answer to a different one — and the
document he picked to make the point, the response on the Privacy and Other
Legislation Amendment Bill 2024, turned out to be missing its Recommendation 1.

Every recovered row went through the same verification as every other row in the
index: found word for word in the document it names, under its own number.

**The correction does not flatter the site.** Coverage moves from 29 per cent to
30 — what was missing was spread across answered and unanswered alike. The
figure that changes most is the size of the index, not the share of it the
government answered.

Full account: toohardbasket.org.au/corrections/ (14 September 2026).

## 2. The register moved

The weekly rebuild ran on 15 September and the site is now on edition 3,
responses harvested to 9 September. Since the edition the brief was written
from, three reports left the Senate register and two left the House register,
and three more responses were read. That is the register working, not an error —
but it moves figures the brief states, so they are listed below too.

2026 documents row reads **24 per cent** rather than 25 (51 of 209), the bills

The last row of the table is not a number. "Harvested to" is the newest tabling
date the harvest holds; it is not a guarantee that everything tabled by then has
been seen, because the Tabled Documents register publishes a response some days
after it is tabled.

## Reproduce this

    git clone https://github.com/toohardbasket-lab/toohardbasket
    python scraper/check_brief.py 2026-09-08

The brief's text and a list of its claims are in `docs/briefs/`. The command
prints the table above from the current dataset. It is the command that produced
it, on 15 September 2026.

A figure moving because the register moved is not a correction; everything under
heading 2 above is that. The 337 missing recommendations are a correction: the
site was wrong, and it is listed, dated and explained at
toohardbasket.org.au/corrections/.

Each edition is kept unchanged at its own address, so a figure quoted from a
particular day can always be checked against the day it was quoted from:
toohardbasket.org.au/as-at/2026-09-15/.
