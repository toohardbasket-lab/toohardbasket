*The Too Hard Basket · Australian Public Interest Alliance · issued 13 September 2026*

The brief invites you to check it: *"Run it and diff the result against this
document."* Doing that today turns up eleven figures that have moved and one
phrase that should be reworded. None of them was wrong when the brief was
written. All of the figure changes come from two corrections to the site made
on 8 and 9 September, both listed at toohardbasket.org.au/corrections/ — the
verdict test was reading "The Government supports…" but not "The Government
does not support…", and recommendations written by a dissenting or minority
author are now counted separately from the committee's, because the government
does not owe the committee an answer on those.

## How this was produced, and how to reproduce it

    git clone https://github.com/toohardbasket-lab/toohardbasket
    python scraper/check_brief.py 2026-09-08

The brief's text and a list of its claims are in `docs/briefs/`. The script
runs `brief_figures.py` against the current dataset, puts each figure back into
the sentence it came from, and prints the ones that no longer agree. It is the
same command that produced the table above, and it will produce a longer table
next month, because the register keeps moving. That is the register working.

A figure moving is not a correction. A correction is the site having been
wrong, and those are listed, dated and explained at
toohardbasket.org.au/corrections/.
