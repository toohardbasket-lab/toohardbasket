Australian Government response to the
Senate Economics Legislation Committee report:
National Energy Transition Authority Bill 2022
JUNE 2026
Introduction
On 27 September 2022, the National Energy Transition Authority Bill 2022 (the bill) was
introduced into the Senate and subsequently referred to the Senate Economics Legislation
Committee (the committee) for inquiry and report. The committee tabled its final report on
24 March 2023.
The bill sought to establish the National Energy Transition Authority (NETA) as an
independent statutory authority with powers and functions to guide Australia's energy
transition and support affected workers and their communities. The proposed authority would
have advisory and law reform functions, including workforce planning, regional economic
development support, and coordination across Commonwealth, state and territory
governments.
The committee received 34 submissions and held a public hearing in Canberra on 28
February 2023, hearing from key stakeholders including trade unions, community
organisations, energy sector representatives, and the Department of Prime Minister and
Cabinet's Net Zero Economy Taskforce.
Key issues examined during the inquiry included the proposed authority's main functions and
powers, support mechanisms for regional communities and workers, potential overlap with
established agencies and existing government functions, the need for worker, union and
First Nations representation, training and re-skilling provisions, and lessons from the Net
Zero Economy Taskforce consultation process.
Committee findings
The committee concluded that establishing NETA would be premature given the ongoing
work of the Net Zero Economy Taskforce. The committee noted that any Transition Authority
should have a clear coordination role and include local trade union, First Nations, industry
and community expertise. The committee also expressed concern that the bill could lead to
duplication of existing government functions.
Coalition Senators tabled additional comments supporting the majority recommendation,
noting concerns about potential overlap with existing state and federal bodies, and
advocating for market-led investment. Senator Penny Allman-Payne (Australian Greens)
tabled a dissenting report recommending that the bill pass and that funding be provided for
the establishment of NETA.
Response
Australian Greens - Recommendation 1
Funding be provided for the establishment of a National Energy Transition Authority in the
forthcoming federal budget in May 2023.
Response
The Australian Government notes this recommendation and notes the establishment of the
Net Zero Economy Authority and relevant policies that have been introduced since the
committee report was completed.
