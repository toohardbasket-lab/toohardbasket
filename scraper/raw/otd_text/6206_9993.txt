RESPONSE TO OMBUDSMAN’S ASSESSMENT NO. 6 / 2024 MADE UNDER
SECTION 486O OF THE MIGRATION ACT 1958
STATEMENT TO PARLIAMENT - No. 6 / 2024
General Comments
I refer to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessment tabled in Parliament today.
This assessment refers to 11 people (10 cases) who have been in immigration
detention for two or more years. This assessment contains recommendations relating
to 3 cases.
Response to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessments
1. Tabling statement for case: 1003337-O3.
The Department of Home Affairs (the Department) will refer this couple’s case for my
consideration under section 195A of the Migration Act 1958 (the Act).
2. Tabling statement for case: 1003856-O1.
The Department will refer this individual’s case for my consideration under sections
195A and 197AB of the Act.
3. Tabling statement for case: 1004078-O.
The Department will refer this individual’s case for my consideration under sections
195A and 197AB of the Act.
4. Tabling statement for cases: 1002265-O5, 1003519-O2, 1003521-O2,
1003559-O2, 1003848-O1, 1004079-O and 1004138-O.
I note that the Ombudsman has made no recommendations in relation to these cases.
