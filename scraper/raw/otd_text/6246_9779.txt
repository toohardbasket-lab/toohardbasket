Overview
The report into the Air Services Amendment Bill was tabled by the Senate Rural and Regional Affairs
and Transport Legislation Committee on 16 August 2018. The Government’s response to the
Report’s recommendations is below.
Australian Government response
The Australian Government’s response to the Air Services Amendment Bill 2018 is set out in detail
below.
Recommendation 1:
The Committee does not support the passage of this bill.
The Government notes this recommendation. However, given the passage of time since this report
was tabled, a substantive Government response is no longer appropriate.
Australian Greens Recommendation 1:
That if the Bill passes the Parliament, a comprehensive review of the adequacy of air noise related
consultation, advocacy and the operation of the Air Noise Ombudsman begin 12 months after the
date of Royal Assent.
The Government notes this recommendation. However, given the passage of time since this report
was tabled, a substantive Government response is no longer appropriate.
Australian Greens Recommendation 2:
That the Air Services Amendment Bill 2018 be passed.
The Government notes this recommendation. However, given the passage of time since this report
was tabled, a substantive Government response is no longer appropriate.
