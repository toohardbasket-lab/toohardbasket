RESPONSE TO OMBUDSMAN’S ASSESSMENTS NO. 8 / 2022 MADE UNDER
SECTION 486O OF THE MIGRATION ACT 1958
STATEMENT TO PARLIAMENT - No. 8 / 2022
General Comments
I refer to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessments tabled in Parliament today.
The assessments refer to 10 people who have been in immigration detention for two or
more years. The assessments contain recommendations relating to four cases.
Response to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessments
1. Tabling statement for case: 1002368-O7.
Portfolio Ministers have personal intervention powers under the Act that allow them to
intervene under section 195A of the Act to grant a visa or under section 197AB of the
Act to make a Residence Determination to allow a person to reside in the community at
a specified address, if they think it is in the public interest to do so.
The personal intervention powers are non-compellable, that is, Portfolio Ministers are
not required to exercise these powers. Portfolio Ministers are not bound by a timeframe
to exercise these powers and it is for Portfolio Ministers to define what is in the public
interest.
The individual’s case was found to not meet the Minister’s section 195A guidelines and
was not referred to a Minister.
This individual was voluntarily removed from Australia.
2. Tabling statement for case: 1002741-O4.
I note the recommendations of the Commonwealth Ombudsman. This person’s transfer
to the relevant immigration detention facility was not considered as they were voluntarily
removed from Australia.
3. Tabling statement for cases: 1003641-O and1003642-O.
I note the recommendations of the Commonwealth Ombudsman. The individuals in
these cases departed Australia under the United States of America Resettlement
Scheme.
4. Tabling statement for cases: 1002608-O4, 1002884-O4, 1003477-O, 1003577-O,
1003666-O and 1003700-O.
I note that the Ombudsman has made no recommendations in relation to these cases.
