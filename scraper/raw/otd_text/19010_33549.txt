Recommendation 1
2.41
The Committee recommends that the House of Representatives resolve, pursuant to Section 18(7) of
the Public Works Committee Act 1969, that it is expedient to carry out the following proposed works:
Department of Defence - Cocos (Keeling) Islands Airfield Upgrade Project.
Response:
The Government agrees to this recommendation.
The Hon Dr Andrew Leigh MP, then Assistant Minister for Competition, Charities and Treasury,
spoke in the blouse of Representatives on 29 March 2023 following the publication of the
Committee's report and moved: "That in accordance with the provisions of the Public Works
Committee Act 1969, it is expedient to carry out the following proposed work which was referred to
the Parliamentary Standing Committee on Public Works and on which the committee has duly
reported to Parliament: Department of Defence — Cocos (Keeling) Islands Airfield Upgrade Project."
House of Representatives Hansard, 29 March 2023, Public Works Joint Committee - Approval of
Work, page 2649.
The House of Representatives agreed to this expediency motion on 29 March 2023.
Recommendation 2
2.43
The Committee recommends that Defence return to provide the Committee with a project midpoint
briefing. This briefing will report on matters including but not limited to how the project has aligned
with the Defence Strategic Review, how the project's budget has been affected by the current
economic climate and a report on ongoing community consultation.
Response:
The Government agrees to this recommendation.
Defence provided a midpoint briefing to the Committee on 10 September 2026 at
Australian Parliament House in Canberra.
