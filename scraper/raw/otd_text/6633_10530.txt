Australian Government response to the
Senate Committee on Economics report:
Treasury Laws Amendment (Cyclone and Flood Damage
Reinsurance Pool) Bill 2022 [Provisions]
May 2024

Response to the recommendations
Additional comments from the Australian Greens - Recommendation 1
The Committee recommends that the scope of damage covered by the Australian Reinsurance
Pool Corporation (APRC) immediately be expanded to include all flood damage to residential and
small business property.
Australian Government response
The Government notes this recommendation. However, given the passage of time since this
report was tabled, a substantive Government response is no longer appropriate.
Additional comments from the Australian Greens - Recommendation 2
The Committee recommends that the scope of damage covered by the ARPC immediately be
expanded to include all damage to motor vehicles associated with damage to residential and
small business property resulting from cyclones and floods.
Australian Government response
The Government notes this recommendation. However, given the passage of time since this
report was tabled, a substantive Government response is no longer appropriate.
Additional comments from the Australian Greens - Recommendation 3
The Committee recommends that the scope of damage covered by the ARPC eventually be
expanded to include all damage to residential and small business property resulting from climatic
events.
Australian Government response
The Government notes this recommendation. However, given the passage of time since this
report was tabled, a substantive Government response is no longer appropriate.
Additional comments from the Australian Greens - Recommendation 4
The Committee recommends that the statutory review of the APRC specifically examine the
expansion of the scope of damage covered by the APRC to include all damage to residential and
small business property resulting from climatic events.
Australian Government response
The Government notes this recommendation. However, given the passage of time since this
report was tabled, a substantive Government response is no longer appropriate.
