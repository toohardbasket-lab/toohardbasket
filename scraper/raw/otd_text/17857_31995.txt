Report 477: Commonwealth Financial Statements – Second
Report
July 2026
Response
Recommendation 1
2.9 The Committee recommends that:
x the Northern Land Council report back to the Committee on progress in responding
to the two significant legislative breaches reported by the Australian National
Audit Office (ANAO) in Audit Report No. 24 (2017-18)
x should the ANAO audit of the financial statements of the Council for the period
ended 30 June 2018 result in any significant legislative breaches being
reported, whether new or unresolved from 2016-17, the Council promptly report
back to the Committee on progress in responding to such findings.
Response
The Northern Land Council (NLC) notes the recommendations. The NLC has provided
responses to later audit reports (specifically Report 488 in 2022) that provides the necessary
update to the Joint Committee of Public Accounts and Audit on the NLCs actions to address
recommendation 477 of report No. 24.
