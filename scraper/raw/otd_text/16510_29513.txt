Australian Government response to the
Senate Appropriations, Staffing and Security Committee
69th report Drafting services for non-government senators
May 2026
Introduction
The Government welcomes the opportunity to respond to the Senate Appropriations, Staffing
and Security Committee (Committee) report titled Drafting services for non-government
senators (the Report).
The Government notes that the Committee endorses the approaches for determining the
allocation of resources outlined in the Clerk’s paper and that the Committee intends to keep a
watching brief on this issue.
The Government provides the following response to the Committee’s recommendations.
Response to Recommendation
Australian Greens Recommendation 1
That the budget of the Department of the Senate be expanded in the 2026-27 Budget to
provide for additional drafting capacity for non-government Senators.
Response: The Government notes the recommendation.
The Government notes that all government entities, including the Department of the Senate,
are responsible for the management and allocation of resources to ensure responsibilities are
carried out efficiently and effectively. Where an additional need or responsibility is identified
that cannot be delivered within resources, entities should engage with the responsible
Minister, or in the case of the Parliamentary departments, the relevant Presiding Officer, who
may consider seeking authority to bring forward a proposal for Government’s consideration
in line with normal budget processes.
