Introduction
On 31 March 2022, the Joint Committee of Public Accounts and Audit
(the Committee) tabled Report 488 – Commonwealth Financial Statements
2019-20 (Report 488).
Government Response to Recommendation 1
Recommendation 1:
The Committee recommends that the Department of the Prime Minister
and Cabinet, in consultation with the Australian National Audit Office,
amend the Auditor-General Act 1997 to implement a requirement for the
Auditor-General to inform Ministers in the event where either a significant
finding or a significant legislative breach is identified during the course of
the financial audits.
Response: Noted
The Government notes this recommendation. However, given the passage of
time since this report was tabled, a substantive Government response is no
longer appropriate.
