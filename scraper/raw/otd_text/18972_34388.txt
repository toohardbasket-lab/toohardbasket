Recommendation 2: The comm¡ttee recommends that the Department of Home Affairs consider
making information on offshore processing contracting arrangements more publicly accessible,
noting commercial-in-confidence considerations and other sensitivities.
The Australian Government agrees to this recommendation.
The Department publishes performance information relating to regional processing arrangements
through its annual corporate reporting, including reporting against corporate plan targets.
Key contract information is published on AusTender, and any significant procurements are included
in the Department's annual procurement plan.
The Department recognises the importance of transparency in government operations. All regional
processing procurements are conducted in accordance with the Commonwealth Procurement Rules
and departmental policies and procedures. At the same time, contracts with service providers
contain confidentiality provisions that reflect the sensitivity of operational and bilateral
arrangements, as well as commercial considerations.
As with all requests for information relating to regional processing and the contracts that support
these operations, the Department will continue to balance transparency obligations with public
interest considerations when determining whether information should be released.
