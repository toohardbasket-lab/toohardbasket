RESPONSE TO OMBUDSMAN’S ASSESSMENT NO. 7 / 2022 MADE UNDER
SECTION 486O OF THE MIGRATION ACT 1958
STATEMENT TO PARLIAMENT - No. 7 / 2022
General Comments
I refer to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessment tabled in Parliament today.
This assessment refers to 10 people who have been in immigration detention for two or
more years. This assessment contains recommendations relating to three cases.
Response to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessment
1. Tabling statement for case: 1002735-O3.
Placement decisions are made following a thorough assessment of the risks associated
with detainee safety and security and consideration of a detainee’s health and welfare
needs. The Department of Home Affairs (the Department) has a robust framework in
place to ensure consistent placement assessments and to determine the most
appropriate placement arrangement for a detainee.
Under this framework, when assessing detainees for transfer to another Immigration
Detention Facility, International Health and Medical Services (IHMS) may provide
advice on the suitability of any transfer based on clinical considerations and
requirements.
This individual has been previously transferred between detention facilities. IHMS did
not advise that any of these transfers would adversely affect the individual’s mental
health. The Department will consult with IHMS and consider any clinical advice when
deciding on any future transfers for this person.
2. Tabling statement for case: 1003219-O1.
This individual was granted a visa and now resides lawfully in the Australian
community.
3. Tabling statement for case: 1003597-O.
The Department previously referred a first stage submission to the former Minister for
Immigration, Citizenship, Migrant Services and Multicultural Affairs under sections 195A
and 197AB of the Migration Act 1958. This submission was returned without decision
for closure following the change in Government.
The Department is now expediting a first stage submission for referral to the Minister
under sections 195A and 197AB of the Act.
4. Tabling statement for cases: 1002865-O3, 1002987-O3, 1003108-O2,
1003185-O1, 1003205-O1, 1003572-O and 1003610-O.
I note that the Ombudsman has made no recommendations in relation to these cases.
