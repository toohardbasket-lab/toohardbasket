Australian Government response to the
House of Representatives Standing Committee on
Procedure report:
Division required? Electronic voting in the House of
Representatives
May 2024
Recommendation 1
The Committee recommends that electronic voting to record divisions in the House of
Representatives be implemented, provided that:
• voting occurs in the Chamber;
• the House maintains the practice of dividing to the right and left of the Chair;
• Members may sit anywhere on the side of the Chamber corresponding to their vote; and
• tellers continue to be appointed to report proxy votes for nursing mothers and to be
ready to undertake a manual tally in the event that the voting system malfunctions.
Australian Government Response
The recommendation has been partially implemented: the votes of Members are recorded
electronically by tellers’ via ipads into the House Division Recording System and
published on screen in the Chamber and the public galleries and on the APH website.
Recommendation 2
The Committee recommends that the option of using a swipe or touch card (or electronic
token) and readers that allow Members to vote from any seat to the left or right of the Chair
be investigated.
Australian Government Response
The Government notes the recommendation.
Recommendation 3
The Committee recommends that the results of divisions be displayed in the Chamber.
Australian Government Response
This recommendation has been implemented.
