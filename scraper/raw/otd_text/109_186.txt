RESPONSE TO OMBUDSMAN’S ASSESSMENT NO. 17 / 2022 MADE UNDER
SECTION 486O OF THE MIGRATION ACT 1958
STATEMENT TO PARLIAMENT - No. 17 / 2022
General Comments
I refer to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessment tabled in Parliament today.
This assessment refers to 10 people who have been in immigration detention for two or
more years. This assessment contains recommendations relating to one case.
Response to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessment
1. Tabling statement for case: 1002022-O4.
Portfolio Ministers have personal intervention powers under the Migration Act 1958
(the Act) that allow them to intervene under section 195A of the Act to grant a visa or
under section 197AB of the Act to make a Residence Determination to allow a person
to reside in the community at a specified address, if they think it is in the public interest
to do so.
The public interest powers are non-compellable, that is, Portfolio Ministers are not
required to exercise these powers. Portfolio Ministers are not bound by a timeframe to
exercise these powers and it is for Portfolio Ministers to define what is in the public
interest.
The former Minister for Immigration, Citizenship, Migrant Services and Multicultural
Affairs intervened under section 197AB of the Act to make a Residence Determination
to allow this person to reside in the community at a specified address.
While this person is residing in the community at a specified address under a
Residence Determination, it is no longer an option to transfer them to the relevant
facility.
2. Tabling statement for cases: 1001366-O, 1003071-O2, 1003238-O1, 1003506-O,
1003623-O, 1003680-O, 1003719-O, 1003721-O, 1003730-O.
I note that the Ombudsman has made no recommendations in relation to these cases.
