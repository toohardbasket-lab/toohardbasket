RESPONSE TO OMBUDSMAN’S ASSESSMENTS NO. 15 / 2022 MADE UNDER
SECTION 486O OF THE MIGRATION ACT 1958
STATEMENT TO PARLIAMENT - No. 15 / 2022
General Comments
I refer to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessments tabled in Parliament today.
These assessments refer to ten people who have been in immigration detention for two
or more years. The assessments contains one recommendation relating to one case.
Response to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessments
1. Tabling statement for case: 1001805-O5
Portfolio Ministers have personal intervention powers under the Migration Act 1958 (the
Act) that allow them to intervene under section 195A of the Act to grant a visa or under
section 197AB of the Act to make a Residence Determination to allow a person to
reside in the community at a specified address, if they think it is in the public interest to
do so.
The public interest powers are non-compellable, that is, Portfolio Ministers are not
required to exercise these powers. Portfolio Ministers are not bound by a timeframe to
exercise these powers and it is for Portfolio Ministers to define what is in the public
interest.
This person has been found to not meet the Minister’s sections 195A and 197AB
guidelines for referral to the Minister. As they do not meet the guidelines, they will not
be referred for Ministerial consideration under section 195A or section 197AB of the
Act.
2. Tabling statement for cases: 1002691-O5, 1003086-O2, 1003360-O1,
1003578-O, 1003602-O, 1003650-O, 1003654-O, 1003701-O and 1003731-O.
I note that the Ombudsman has made no recommendations in relation to these cases.
