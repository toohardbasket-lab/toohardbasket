Introduction
On 20 November 2014, the Joint Standing Committee on Electoral Matters (JSCEM) tabled a
report titled Second interim report on the inquiry into the conduct of the 2013 federal
election: An assessment of electronic voting options.
The Government’s response to the recommendations of the Report follows below.
