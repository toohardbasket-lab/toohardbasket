RESPONSE TO OMBUDSMAN’S ASSESSMENT NO. 7 / 2024 MADE UNDER
SECTION 486O OF THE MIGRATION ACT 1958
STATEMENT TO PARLIAMENT - No. 7 / 2024
General Comments
I refer to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessment tabled in Parliament today.
This assessment refers to 10 people who have been in immigration detention for two or
more years. This assessment contains recommendations relating to three cases.
Response to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessments
1. Tabling statement for case: 1003340-O2.
On 18 March 2024, a first stage submission for this individual’s case was referred for
my consideration under sections 195A and 197AB of the Migration Act 1958 (the Act).
2. Tabling statement for case: 1003518-O2.
The Department of Home Affairs (the Department) agrees to reconsider this individual’s
placement in line with standard detention operational policy and procedures.
The Department will refer this individual’s case for my consideration under sections
195A and/or 197AB of the Act.
3. Tabling statement for case: 1004110-O.
On 24 April 2024, the Department transferred the individual in this case to a location
closer to their family and support networks.
4. Tabling statement for cases: 1003063-O4, 1003883-O1, 1004100-O, 1004109-O,
1004124-O, 1004126-O and 1004127-O.
I note that the Ombudsman has made no recommendations in relation to these cases.
(Original signed by Andrew Giles)
ANDREW GILES
Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs
16 / 05 / 2024
