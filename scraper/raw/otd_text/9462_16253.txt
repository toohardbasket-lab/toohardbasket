Speaker’s response to recommendation 3 of the House Standing Committee on
Procedure’s report, Maintenance of the standing and sessional orders

The Speaker welcomes the Procedure Committee’s November 2024 report, Maintenance of
the standing and sessional orders.

Most of the recommendations in the report concern proposed changes to the standing
orders and practices and procedures of the House (recommendations 1, 2, 4, 5), which are a
matter for the House and are expected to be addressed in the Government response. This
response does not address those recommendations.

The Speaker's response to recommendation 3 is as follows:
Recommendation 3:

4.13. The Committee recommends that, subject to feasibility and reasonable cost, minor
cosmetic changes be made to the Federation Chamber to befit its status as a second
Chamber of the House and less like a committee room.

Response

The Speaker supports this recommendation to enhance the status of the Federation
Chamber, subject to cost and time constraints, and the outcome of consultation with
stakeholders.

The Speaker will ask the Department of the House of Representatives to consult with the
Department of Parliamentary Services and other stakeholders to develop advice on potential
options (including with respect to feasibility and cost), for minor changes, such as to wall
panelling and chair leather in the Federation Chamber, to match the shades of green in the
House of Representatives Chamber (noting the considerations of the Committee in the
report).
