Dear President

I am writing to convey the Government’s response to the former Senate Select Committee on the
Establishment of a National Integrity Commission’s /nterim Report, which predates the
47" Parliament.

The Government notes each recommendation of the report. However, given the passage of time
since the report was tabled, a substantive Government response is no longer appropriate.

The Government notes that the National Anti-Corruption Commission Act 2022 established the
National Anti-Corruption Commission, which commenced operations on 1 July 2023.

The National Anti-Corruption Commission is an independent Commonwealth agency able to
detect, investigate and report on serious or systemic corrupt conduct in the Commonwealth
public sector.

Please accept this letter as the Government’s response to the Jnterim Report. A copy of this letter
has also been provided to the Senior Clerk’s Office, which is responsible for the secretariat
functions of the former Senate Select Committee on the Establishment of a National Integrity
Commission.
