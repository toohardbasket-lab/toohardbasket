Australian Government response to the
Senate Committee on Environment and
Communications report:
Competition and Consumer Amendment (Prevention of
Exploitation of Indigenous Cultural Expressions) Bill 2019
May 2024

Response to the recommendations
Recommendation 1
The committee recommends that the Commonwealth consult Indigenous artists, organisations
and communities to develop legislation to prohibit the sale of inauthentic Indigenous products
sold as souvenirs, either through amendment of the Competition and Consumer Act 2010 or
through another mechanism.
Australian Government response
The Government notes this recommendation. However, given the passage of time since this
report was tabled, a substantive Government response is no longer appropriate.
Labor Senators' dissenting report - Recommendation 1
Labor Senators call on the Government to respond to the 2018 HRSCIA ‘Report on the impact of
inauthentic art and craft in the style of First Nations peoples’, as a matter of urgency.
Australian Government response
The Government notes this recommendation. However, given the passage of time since this
report was tabled, a substantive Government response is no longer appropriate.
Labor Senators' dissenting report - Recommendation 2
Labor Senators call on the Government to work with First Nations people on a comprehensive
legislative framework to prevent exploitation through inauthentic art, craft and cultural
expression, and address deficiencies with the current Bill; consistent with the recommendations
of the HRSCIA report.
Australian Government response
The Government notes this recommendation. However, given the passage of time since this
report was tabled, a substantive Government response is no longer appropriate.
