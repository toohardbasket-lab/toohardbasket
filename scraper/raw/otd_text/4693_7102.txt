Australian Government response to the
Legal and Constitutional Affairs Legislation Committee report:
Migration Amendment (Evacuation to Safety) Bill 2023
August 2023
Introduction
On 7 February 2023, the Migration Amendment (Evacuation to Safety) Bill 2023 (the Bill) was
introduced in the Senate by Senator Nick McKim. The Bill would require the Australian
government to offer people taken to Papua New Guinea (PNG) or Nauru for regional
processing the opportunity to transfer to Australia, provided they are not subject to an adverse
security assessment.
On 9 February 2023, on the recommendation of the Senate Selection of Bills Committee, the
Bill was referred to the Legal and Constitutional Affairs Legislation Committee (the
Committee) for inquiry and report by 7 March 2023.
On 7 March 2023, the Final Report was tabled in Parliament detailing the history of regional
processing arrangements, and the criticality of maintaining these arrangements to deter people
smugglers from exploiting and encouraging vulnerable people to risk their lives at sea. The
Committee recommended the Senate not pass the Bill.
On 8 March 2023, the Bill was defeated in the Senate by a vote of 24 to 12.
Response
Committee recommendation
Recommendation 1: The Government agrees with the recommendation of the Committee that
the Senate does not pass the Bill that was tabled in Parliament.
Dissenting report from the Australian Greens
Recommendation 2: The Government does not accept the recommendation of the Australian
Greens that the Senate pass the Bill.
Recommendation 3: The Government does not accept the recommendation of the Australian
Greens that the Parliament repeals the statutory basis for the government’s offshore
processing regime, and that the government terminates all agreements and tenders for
regional processing, and dismantles Operation Sovereign Borders.
