Introduction
On 28 March 2023, the Senate referred an inquiry into the administration of the referendum
into an Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Voice to the Senate Finance and Public
Administration References Committee (the Committee).
On 9 June 2023, the Committee tabled a report titled Administration of the referendum into
an Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Voice.
The Government’s formal response to the recommendation of the Report follows below.
