Introduction
On 15 April 2015, the Joint Standing Committee on Electoral Matters (JSCEM) tabled a
report titled The 2013 Federal Election: Report on the conduct of the 2013 election and
matters related thereto.
The Government’s response to the recommendations of the Report follows below.

elections and referenda.
