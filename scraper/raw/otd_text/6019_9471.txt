Australian Government response to the
Senate Standing Committee on Legal and Constitutional
Affairs report:
Nationhood, national identity and democracy
MAY 2024
Overview
On 29 July 2019 the Senate referred an inquiry into nationhood, national identity and
democracy to the Legal and Constitutional Affairs References Committee. The report was
tabled on 16 February 2021.
Government Response
The Government notes all recommendations. However, given the passage of time since this
report was tabled, a substantive Government response is no longer appropriate.
