Dear President

I am writing to convey the Government’s response to the former Joint Committee on the
Australian Commission for Law Enforcement Integrity’s report on the /nquiry into the
Jurisdiction of the Australian Commission for Law Enforcement Integrity, which predates the
47" Parliament.

The Government notes each recommendation of the report. However, given the passage of time
since the report was tabled, a substantive Government response is no longer appropriate.

I would also like to take to note that The National Anti-Corruption Commission Act 2022
established the National Anti-Corruption Commission and replaced the Law Enforcement
Integrity Commissioner Act 2006. The National Anti-Corruption Commission commenced
operations on | July 2023. The National Anti-Corruption Commission is an independent
Commonwealth agency able to detect, investigate and report on serious or systemic corrupt
conduct in the Commonwealth public sector

Please accept this letter as the Government’s response to the /nquiry into the jurisdiction of the
Australian Commission for Law Enforcement Integrity report. A copy of this letter has also been
provided to the Hon Milton Dick MP, Speaker of the House of Representatives, and to the Senior
Clerk’s Office, which is responsible for the secretariat functions of the former Joint Committee
on the Australian Commission for Law Enforcement Integrity.

Yours sincerely
