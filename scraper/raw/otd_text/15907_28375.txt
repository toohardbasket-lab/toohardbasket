Australian Government response to the
The Joint Select Committee on Constitutional Recognition relating to
Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Peoples report
APRIL 2026
OVERVIEW
On 19 March 2018, the Parliament agreed that a Joint Select Committee on Constitutional Recognition relating to Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander
People be appointed to inquire into and report on matters relating to constitutional change, including the proposal for the establishment of a First Nations
Voice. The final report was presented 29 November 2018.
GOVERNMENT RESPONSE
The Government notes the recommendations. Given the passage of time since the report, the Government provides the following update:
The Government delivered on its commitment to hold a referendum on constitutional recognition through a Voice to Parliament on Saturday 14 October
2023.
