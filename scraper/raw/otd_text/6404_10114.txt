Australian Government response to the
Joint Select Committee on the Australia Fund
Establishment report:
An inquiry into the establishment of a fund to support
rural and manufacturing industries
May 2024
Recommendation 1
The committee recommends an evaluation of current concessional loan schemes to ensure
that the schemes are utilising appropriate data and that eligibility criteria are designed in
consultation with the sector to ensure that schemes are as accessible as possible for those in
need of Commonwealth government assistance.
Australian Government Response
The Government notes this recommendation. However, given the passage of time since this
report was tabled, a substantive Government response is no longer appropriate.
Recommendation 2
The committee recommends that the Commonwealth government consider the
development of a land management technology fund to support the rural sector, and to
ensure that the sector is equipped with appropriate Commonwealth government managed
data.

Recommendation 3
The committee recommends that the Commonwealth government works with state and
territory governments to ensure that the seafood and aquaculture industry is an integral
