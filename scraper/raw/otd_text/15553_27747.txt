Dear President

I am writing to convey the Government’s response to the former Parliamentary Joint Committee
on the Australian Commission for Law Enforcement Integrity’s report on the Examination of the
Annual Report of the Integrity Commissioner 2020-21, which predates the 47" Parliament.

The Government notes each recommendation of the report. However, given the passage of time
since the report was tabled, a substantive Government response is no longer appropriate.

Please accept this letter as the Government’s response to the report on the Examination of the
Annual Report of the Integrity Commissioner 2020-21. A copy of this letter has also been
provided to the Hon Milton Dick MP, Speaker of the House of Representatives, and to the Senior
Clerk’s Office, which is responsible for the secretariat functions of the former Parliamentary
Joint Committee on the Australian Commission for Law Enforcement Integrity.

Yours sincerely

An, Qe
