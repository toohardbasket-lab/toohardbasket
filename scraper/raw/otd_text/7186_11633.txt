Australian Government response to the
Parliamentary Joint Committee on Intelligence and Security report:
Advisory report on the amendments made by the Australian
Citizenship Amendment (Citizenship Repudiation) Bill 2023
August 2024
Recommendations
Recommendation 1: the Government consider any potential amendments to the new
cessation regime in the Australian Citizenship Act 2007 after a reasonable period of 18-24
months so that its operation and effectiveness can be assessed.
Response: The Government accepts this recommendation.
The Government agrees that 18-24 months is a reasonable period to allow before considering
potential amendments to the citizenship cessation regime. However, the Government may
consider amendments within a shorter time frame if necessary to protect Australia’s national
security.
Recommendation 2: should the Government consider any potential amendments to the new
cessation regime after 18-24 months, the Committee recommends that the Government:
 Further consider the safeguards against rendering a person stateless with the
Australian Citizenship Act 2007, to ensure that the provisions prevent the possibility
of a person being left stateless, including in a de facto sense where they are unable to
effectively exercise the rights of citizenship of the other country.
 Reconsider the inclusion of children aged 14-17 in the citizenship cessation regime
and, if application of the regime to children is considered absolutely necessary, the
Australian Citizenship Act 2007 be amended so that it is clear that a court may only
make a citizenship cessation order in exceptional circumstances.
Response: The Government accepts this recommendation.
Noting that in paragraph 3.8 of the report the Committee is generally satisfied with the
safeguards provided by the Australian Citizenship Amendment (Citizenship Repudiation) Act
2023, the Government agrees to consider amendments, if required and after review, to further
ensure against statelessness, including in a de facto sense, and the inclusion of children aged
14-17.
