Australian Government response to the
Senate Standing Committee on Economics report:
Australia’s oil and gas reserves
[June 2024]
The Government notes recommendations 1-2 made by the Committee in it’s report, additional
recommendations 1-9 made by former Senator Rex Patrick and recommendation 1 made in the
Dissenting report by Australian Greens Senators. However, given the passage of time since this
report was tabled, a substantive Government response to the recommendations is no longer
appropriate.
