Dear Minister

I write in relation to the compliance motion moved by Senator McKenzie, agreed to by the Senate on
Wednesday, 26 November 2025, requiring the Minister representing the Treasurer, by no later than 12:00pm
on Thursday, 4 December 2025 to present the Government’s response to the report of the Rural and Regional
Affairs and Transport References Committee, Bank closures in regional Australia.

As previously advised, since the References Committee tabled its report, the Government has announced, on
11 February 2025, major bank moratoriums on regional branch closures until 31 July 2027 and new
in-principle Bank@Post agreements between Australia Post and the major banks.

As an update, I am pleased to report that, on 16 November 2025, Westpac Banking Corporation announced
that it is extending its moratorium on regional branch closures until 2030. I am also able to report that all
four major banks have now entered into new Bank@Post agreements with Australia Post.

Also, as previously advised, the Council of Financial Regulators (CFR) and the Australian Competition and
Consumer Commission (ACCC), on 16 July 2025, released a paper for public consultation on regulating cash
distribution in Australia, including ensuring cash continues to be distributed to regional and remote locations.
The consultation closed on 15 August 2025. As an update, the CFR and ACCC have provided a conclusions
paper to the Government and the Government is considering next steps.

The Government is also continuing to work with the banking sector and other key stakeholders on long term
solutions to regional banking, and will formally respond to the report in due course.

I would appreciate you conveying this response to the President of the Senate.
