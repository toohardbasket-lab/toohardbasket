RESPONSE TO OMBUDSMAN’S ASSESSMENT NO. 22 / 2022 MADE UNDER
SECTION 486O OF THE MIGRATION ACT 1958
STATEMENT TO PARLIAMENT - No. 22 / 2022
General Comments
I refer to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessments tabled in Parliament today.
The assessments refer to 10 people who have been in immigration detention for two or
more years. The assessments contain recommendations relating to two cases.
Response to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessments
1. Tabling statement for case: 1003075-O2.
This person is subject to a positive protection finding under section 197C(5)(a) of the
Act.
As a result of that protection finding, it is no longer necessary for the Department to
undertake an ITOA to assess this person’s protection claims.
2. Tabling statement for case: 1003662-O.
The Minister exercised his power under section 195A of the Migration Act 1958 to grant
a visa to this person.
3. Tabling statement for cases: 1002790-O4, 1003093-O2, 1003631-O, 1003703-O,
1003707-O, 1003715-O, 1003718-O, 1003769-O.
I note that the Ombudsman has made no recommendations in relation to these cases.
(Original signed by Andrew Giles)
ANDREW GILES
Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs
17 / 08 / 2022
