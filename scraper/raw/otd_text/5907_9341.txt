Introduction
On 15 May 2020, the Joint Standing Committee on Electoral Matters tabled a report entitled
Interim report on all aspects of the conduct of the 2019 Federal Election and matters related
thereto: Delegation to the International Grand Committee, Dublin, Ireland (the Report).
The Government’s response to the recommendations of the Report follows below.
