Recommendation 1

18(7) of the Public Works Committee Act 1969, that it is expedient to carry out the
following proposed works: Australian Securities & Investments Commission Proposed Fit
Out of New Leased Premises at 100 Market Street, Sydney.

report was tabled, a substantive Government response is no longer appropriate.
