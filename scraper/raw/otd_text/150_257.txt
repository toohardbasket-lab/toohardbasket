GOVERNMENT RESPONSE TO THE SENATE ECONOMICS
REFERENCES COMMITTEE FINAL REPORT

Australia’s sovereign naval shipbuilding capability

The Government welcomes the tabling of the Senate Economics References Committee’s
Final Report on Australia’s sovereign naval shipbuilding capability on 18 May 2022.

The Government supports the longstanding principle of the Parliament inquiring into and
reporting on activities within Commonwealth departments. The Government notes the
recommendations of the final report. The recommendations will be considered as part of the
Defence Strategic Review, and in the development of the roadmap for the delivery of
nuclear-powered submarines for Australia and drafting of an updated Naval Shipbuilding and
Sustainment Plan.

The Government and the Department of Defence are committed to ensuring that Australia has
the maritime capabilities required to fulfil its strategic objectives. Investments across the
naval shipbuilding and sustainment enterprise will continue to ensure the operational
effectiveness of Australia’s naval capability and support thousands of jobs, now and into the
future.
