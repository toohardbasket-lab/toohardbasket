RESPONSE TO OMBUDSMAN’S ASSESSMENT NO. 19 / 2022 MADE UNDER
SECTION 486O OF THE MIGRATION ACT 1958
STATEMENT TO PARLIAMENT - No. 19 / 2022
General Comments
I refer to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessment tabled in Parliament today.
This assessment refers to 10 people who have been in immigration detention for two or
more years. This assessment contains recommendations relating to two cases.
Response to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessment
1. Tabling statement for case: 1001254-O4
The former Minister for Immigration, Citizenship, Migrant Services and Multicultural
Affairs declined to consider this person’s case under section 195A of the Migration Act
1958 (the Act).
2. Tabling statement for case: 1003624-O
The Department notes the Ombudsman’s recommendation. This person is provided
with support to manage their health, wellbeing and personal welfare.
They receive psychiatrist, trauma counsellor and general practitioner review along with
dental and physiotherapy support and ongoing International Health and Medical
Services (IHMS) counselling. Following their most recent medical review, IHMS advised
this person had no outstanding medical or surgical appointments.
3. Tabling statement for cases: 1001148-O6, 1002667-O3, 1002915-O2,
1003087-O2, 1003355-O1, 1003374-O1, 1003423-O1 and 1003756-O.
I note that the Ombudsman has made no recommendations in relation to these cases.
(Original signed by Andrew Giles)
ANDREW GILES
Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs
