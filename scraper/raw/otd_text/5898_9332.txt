Introduction
On 16 October 2020, the Joint Standing Committee on Electoral Matters (JSCEM) tabled a
report entitled Advisory report on the Commonwealth Electoral Amendment (Ensuring Fair
Representation of the Northern Territory) Bill 2020.
The Government’s response to the recommendations of the Report follows below.
Government Response to the Joint Standing Committee on Electoral Matters report: Advisory
report on the Commonwealth Electoral Amendment (Ensuring Fair Representation of the
Northern Territory) Bill 2020.
