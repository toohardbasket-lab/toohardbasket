Introduction
On 9 May 2014, the Joint Standing Committee on Electoral Matters (JSCEM) tabled a report
titled Interim report on the inquiry into the conduct of the 2013 Federal Election: Senate
voting practices.
The Government’s response to the recommendations of the Report follows below.
Government Response to the Joint Standing Committee on Electoral Matters reports:
Interim report on the inquiry into the conduct of the 2013 Federal Election:
