RESPONSE TO OMBUDSMAN’S ASSESSMENT NO. 9 / 2022 MADE UNDER
SECTION 486O OF THE MIGRATION ACT 1958
STATEMENT TO PARLIAMENT - No. 9 / 2022
General Comments
I refer to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessment tabled in Parliament today.
This assessment refers to 10 people who have been in immigration detention for two or
more years. This assessment contains recommendations relating to three cases.
Response to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessments
1. Tabling statement for case: 1002626-O4.
The person in this case is detained under section 189 of the Migration Act 1958 (the
Act) in an Immigration Detention Facility after their transfer from a correctional facility.
The Department is finalising a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the relevant
Corrective Services agency. The MoU will formalise arrangements for the exchange of
information and the provision of Immigration Detention Services through the
accommodation and care of immigration detainees in facilities of the Corrective
Services agency.
2. Tabling statement for case: 1003044-O2.
The person in this case is detained under section 189 of the Migration Act 1958 (the
Act) in an Immigration Detention Facility after their transfer from a correctional facility.
The Department is finalising a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the relevant
Corrective Services agency. The MoU will formalise arrangements for the exchange of
information and the provision of Immigration Detention Services through the
accommodation and care of immigration detainees in facilities of the Corrective
Services agency.
3. Tabling statement for case: 1003201-O1.
Following the Ombudsman’s recommendation, the Department commenced a
Ministerial Intervention process for this person’s case to be assessed against the
Minister’s section 197AB guidelines. This person was found to not meet the Minister’s
section 197AB guidelines for referral to the Minister.
4. Tabling statement for cases: 1002887-O3, 1003541-O, 1003548-O, 1003580-O,
1003586-O, 1003612-O and 1003617-O.
I note that the Ombudsman has made no recommendations in relation to these cases.
