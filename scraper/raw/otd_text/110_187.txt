RESPONSE TO OMBUDSMAN’S ASSESSMENTS NO. 18 / 2022 MADE UNDER
SECTION 486O OF THE MIGRATION ACT 1958
STATEMENT TO PARLIAMENT - No. 18 / 2022
General Comments
I refer to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessments tabled in Parliament today.
These assessments refer to ten people who have been in immigration detention for two
or more years. This assessment contains one recommendation relating to one case.
Response to the Commonwealth Ombudsman’s assessments
1. Tabling statement for case: 1003653-O.
The Department previously referred a first stage submission to the former Minister for
Immigration, Citizenship, Migrant Services and Multicultural Affairs under section 195A
of the Migration Act 1958. This submission was returned without decision for closure
following the change in Government.
The Department is now expediting a first stage submission for referral to the Minister
under section 195A of the Act.
2. Tabling statement for cases: 1001693-O1, 1002359-O6, 1002373-O7,
1002840-O3, 1003539-O1, 1003646-O, 1003728-O, 1003729-O and 1003752-O.
I note that the Ombudsman has made no recommendations in relation to these cases.
(Original signed by Andrew Giles)
ANDREW GILES
Minister for Immigration, Citizenship and Multicultural Affairs
