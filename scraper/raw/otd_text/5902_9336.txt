Introduction
On 21 June 2017, the Joint Standing Committee on Electoral Matters (JSCEM) tabled a report
titled Third interim report on the inquiry into the conduct of the 2016 federal election: AEC
modernisation.
The Government’s response to the recommendations of the Report follows below.
