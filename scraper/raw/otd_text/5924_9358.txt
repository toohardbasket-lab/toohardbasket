Recommendation 1
The Committee affirms the importance of resolving policy uncertainty in relation to
emissions reduction in the electricity sector. The Committee commends to the House the

establishment of a stable and enduring mechanism for scalable emissions reduction in the
electricity sector, with appropriate notice given for changes in targets.

NOTED

Recommendation 7

The Committee recommends that, independent of the Regulatory Investment Test for
Transmission (RIT-T) process, the Australian Energy Market Operator be funded to
undertake further feasibility studies to ascertain whether:

- additional interconnectors are required on the grid and, if so, where; and

- additional transmission is required in certain areas of the grid, including appropriate
planning for renewable energy zones. The Committee also recommends that the relevant
rules, including the RIT-T process, be reviewed to take into account:

- future expansion of the base for generating electricity; and

- emissions reduction in the electricity sector.
