Inquiry into constitutional reform and referendums

Introduction
On 9 February 2022, the House of Representatives Standing Committee on Social Policy and
Legal Affairs tabled a report titled Inquiry into constitutional reform and referendums.
The Government’s response to the recommendations of the Report follows below.
Government Response to the House of Representatives Standing Committee on Social Policy
and Legal Affairs report: Inquiry into constitutional reform and referendums
