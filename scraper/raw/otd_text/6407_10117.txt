Australian Government response to the
Senate Finance and Public Administration Legislation
Committee report:
Plebiscite for an Australian Republic Bill 2008
May 2024
Recommendation 1
The committee recommends the establishment of an ongoing public awareness campaign
on Australia's constitutional system which engages as wide a range of the public as
possible.
Australian Government Response
The Government notes this recommendation. However, given the passage of time since this
report was tabled, a substantive Government response is no longer appropriate.
Recommendation 2
The committee recommends that if any further process advocating constitutional change is
undertaken, including that of a republic, it seek to encourage Australians to engage
meaningfully in the debate.
Australian Government Response
The Government notes this recommendation. However, given the passage of time since this
report was tabled, a substantive Government response is no longer appropriate.
